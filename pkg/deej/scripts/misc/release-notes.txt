deej remix __VERSION__

A remix of deej with a live control panel, profiles and zero-config connecting. Same hardware, same serial protocol: any vanilla deej board works as-is.

**What's new**

- **Control panel:** tray icon → _Open control panel_ (http://localhost:7979). A live mixer view where your pots move on screen as you move them
- **Sliders or knobs**, picked per pot, plus invert, an _audio_ curve for finer low-volume control, and calibration for pots that never reach 0/100%
- **Auto-detect COM port** (`com_port: auto`) and automatic reconnect when the board is unplugged and plugged back in
- **Audio session discovery:** apps playing audio show up live. Drag them onto a pot
- **Profiles:** switch from the panel, the tray or keys 1–9, or let one auto-activate while a game/app is running
- Settings save instantly, and hand edits to `config.yaml` still apply live
- Optional smoother firmware (`deej-remix.ino`) with oversampling + smoothing for cheap pots

**Launch instructions**

- **First-time users:** upload the Arduino sketch to your board. `deej-remix.ino` is included below, and the vanilla sketch works too
- Unzip anywhere and launch `deej.exe` (Windows) or `./deej` (Linux). The control panel opens on first launch
- **Upgrading from vanilla deej:** keep your old `config.yaml` next to the new binary. It's migrated into a `default` profile automatically

**_Linux users:_** the binary needs `libgtk-3` and `libayatana-appindicator3` installed (present on most desktops).

> _Tip:_ If deej seems to crash or doesn't start, check `logs/deej-latest-run.log` next to the binary.

> _Tip:_ `deej-debug.exe` is a version with a console window that displays additional logs.
